About

Capiz, reimagined.

For generations, capiz shells have been polished and pieced together by hand along the coasts of the Visayas. Each Articulo piece carries that craft forward — softly diffused, gently iridescent, and made slowly, the way it has always been made.

We work directly with small workshops to design pieces that feel modern but never disposable.
